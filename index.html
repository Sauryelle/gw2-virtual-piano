  3. Play with your keyboard: 1–8 for the white keys, F1–F6 for sharps.

Scribe

Turn a MIDI file into a tab.

Arrange moves the song onto white keys, keeps the melody and bass in their real octaves and fits every chord in one octave (three notes at most). Duet: melody + accompaniment. Solo: the melody with a chord on every beat, in one octave. + Bass part adds a bassline: the song’s own, or one built from its chords. Mute a part to leave it out.

Jam

Play together, wherever you are.

Not connected offline

Playing together connects you directly to the other players, which means they can see your IP address. Only jam with people you trust — and pick a password nobody else will guess. To hide your address, tick Always go through TURN under “Trouble connecting?”.

Trouble connecting? (VPN, strict firewall)

Players connect straight to each other. Behind a VPN or a strict firewall that direct path often fails, even though you are both in the right room. A TURN server forwards the notes instead. Free ones are tried automatically; if they are busy, paste your own below — Jam only sends a few KB per minute, so any free tier is plenty.
